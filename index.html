Takeover

A visual essay on how one kind of life comes to be run by another. These are the figures drafted so far, in the order they will appear.

  • The arch Part 1, scenes 2 and 4

    How a structure whose parts all need each other gets built.

  • The rope Part 1, scene 5

    How a line of descent changes what it is made of.

  • The takeover Part 1, scene 9

    Cairns-Smith’s scheme: a second gene begins inside the box of the first.

  • The circle closes Part 1, scene 10

    The last step: the new system makes all of its own parts.

  • A loop forms Not yet placed

    A self-feeding loop arising in a mixture. Parked until the autocatalysis reading is done.

  • The race Replicators, not yet placed

    Speed, litter size, lifespan and accuracy, run side by side.

  • The edge Replicators, not yet placed

    Copiers on a surface of fixed size: growth, then the edge, then full.

  • Three closures Replicators, not yet placed

    Parts, power and instructions: what a copier supplies for itself.

The figures follow A. G. Cairns-Smith, Genetic Takeover and the Mineral Origins of Life (Cambridge University Press, 1982). The text on every page is placeholder draft copy.
