The edge

On a surface of fixed size, growth soon belongs to the edge.

A copier needs somewhere to put its copy. On open ground the count multiplies. Once the middle of the patch is packed, only the units on its rim have anywhere to copy into, and the patch grows at the speed the rim can move.

The same figure, at a larger scale, is the last one in this essay.

After Robert A. Freitas Jr. and Ralph C. Merkle, Kinematic Self-Replicating Machines (Landes Bioscience, 2004), section 5.9.3.
