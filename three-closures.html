Three closures

A machine copies itself only as far as it supplies its own parts, power and instructions.

Freitas and Merkle call the share a machine supplies for itself its closure, and count it three ways: matter, energy and information. A machine short of full closure in any one of them copies only while someone else keeps that line supplied.

For an engineer that open line is a safety device. For the argument of this essay it is the scaffolding: the thing the copier still leans on, and the thing that can be dropped once the last line closes.

After Robert A. Freitas Jr. and Ralph C. Merkle, Kinematic Self-Replicating Machines (Landes Bioscience, 2004), sections 5.6 and 5.1.9 A1, E4 and J5.
